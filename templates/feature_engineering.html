Frequency Encoding Rationale:
Features like device_ip (561k categories) and device_id (151k categories) cannot be One-Hot Encoded without creating an unmanageable 700k+ sparse matrix. By replacing each category with its raw count frequency, we capture popularity signals while keeping dimensionality at 23 features.
